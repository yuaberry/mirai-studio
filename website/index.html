Character Studio

Full cast profiles — role, personality, goals, fears, voice — feeding canon-aware AI context and scene casting.

Episodes, Scenes & Screenplay

Season structure with production status; scenes with cast, location, time of day and screenplay text. AI drafts, you approve.

Storyboard Studio

Shots with pro camera metadata — framing, lens, movement, duration — and real frames stored inside your project.

Manga Prompt Library

12 curated professional prompts: manga panels, character sheets, keyframes, style anchors, negatives. Copy or send to AI Assist.

Bring Your Own AI

Chat & drafting via OpenRouter or NVIDIA NIM (GLM 5.3) — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Model discovery with free-tier flags.

Local-first & Portable

SQLite per project, atomic manifests, ULID identity — move your project folder anywhere. Backups that never destroy your only copy.

Consent & Security

Keys encrypted with your OS keychain; only the context you explicitly enable ever leaves the machine — with a clear privacy notice.

Crash-proof Jobs

Every heavy operation is a persistent job with retry, cancel, progress — and recovered automatically after a crash.
